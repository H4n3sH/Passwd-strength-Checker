01

Length matters

Longer passwords and passphrases can be harder to guess. Prefer unique passwords of at least 12–16 characters where practical.

Use unique passwords

A password reused across websites can expose multiple accounts if one service is breached.

03

Use a password manager

A password manager can generate and store strong, unique passwords for your accounts.
